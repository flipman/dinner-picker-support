Tomato & basil pasta

4 servings  ·  5 min prep  ·  20 min cooking

A simple tomato sauce, fresh basil, and your favorite pasta.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta

    Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to the package directions. Reserve 1/2 cup of the cooking water, then drain.

  2. Soften the garlic

    Warm the olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the chopped garlic and stir for 1 minute, until fragrant. Do not let it brown.

  3. Simmer the sauce

    Add the crushed tomatoes and salt. Bring to a gentle simmer. Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  4. Finish and serve

    Add the drained pasta to the sauce. Toss with the basil, loosening the sauce with a little reserved cooking water as needed. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve.
